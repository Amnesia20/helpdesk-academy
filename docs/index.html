How it works

Every ticket is a live call.

The tickets take place at Brightline Logistics, a made-up 800-person company. Every person, phone number and address in it is fictional.

  1. Pick a ticket

    16 tickets from Easy to Hard: lockouts, Outlook, Wi-Fi, printers, MFA, BitLocker, phishing, VIPs, and more.

  2. Talk to the caller

    Each caller has a personality, a mood, and a screen you can't see. They only give clues when you ask the right questions.

  3. Use the tools

    An Admin Center to unlock accounts, reset passwords and MFA, and manage groups. A policy knowledge base. A Senior Tech for hints.

  4. Close it and get graded

    Write your notes, close it as Resolved or Escalated, and get a score with what went well and what to improve.

Grading

Graded the way a team lead would.

Fixing the problem isn't enough. You're scored on how you got there, whether you followed policy, and whether the next tech could act on your notes.

Troubleshooting 25 pts

Logical diagnosis, and evidence before changes.

Resolution 25 pts

Fixed the root cause or escalated to the right team, and confirmed with the caller.

Security & policy 20 pts

Verified identity first, least privilege, protected secrets.

Communication 15 pts

Empathy, plain language, setting expectations.

Documentation 15 pts

Notes the next tech could act on.

Some mistakes end the call

A critical failure, like resetting the CFO's password for a caller you never verified, caps the score at 40, no matter how friendly you were.

Hints cost points

The Senior Tech gives nudges, not answers: up to 3 per ticket, 3 points each.

XP and ranks

XP is your best score times the difficulty (1×, 2× or 3×). Ranks go from Trainee to Help Desk Lead.

Install

Up and running in about five minutes.

No admin rights needed. It installs just for your Windows account.

  1. Download the installer

    HelpDesk-Academy-Setup-1.0.1.exe (118 MB) from GitHub.

  2. Run it

    Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Accept the license, pick a folder, and finish.

  3. Connect an AI service

    Settings opens the first time you start the app. Pick one, click Test connection, then Save and restart.

    Groq FreeSign up at console.groq.com, create an API key, and paste it in. Good for one person or a few students at a time.
    Claude PaidThe most natural callers and grading. Needs prepaid credit at console.anthropic.com.
    Ollama Free, offlineRuns the AI on your own computer. Needs a strong graphics card.

  4. Pick a ticket

    Choose a username and a 4 to 8 digit PIN to create your profile, then start with an Easy ticket. No AI key yet? The app still opens in demo mode so you can look around.

Before you start

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • An internet connection for Groq or Claude.
  • Use made-up details in tickets. Never type real passwords or personal information.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. The app is free, and Groq's free AI tier is enough for one person or a few students at a time. If it gets busy, the app tells you how long to wait. Claude costs a few cents to about 60 cents per ticket, depending on the model.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Windows warns about apps that aren't signed with a paid code-signing certificate. The installer is built straight from the public source code, and you can compare its checksum with the one above. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

The installer is Windows only for now. On Mac or Linux you can run it from the source code with Node.js. The setup guide shows how.

Is anything real in the tickets?

No. Brightline Logistics and everyone in it are made up. Use made-up details when you answer too, and never type a real password.

Where is my progress saved? Will I lose it if I update?

In your Windows profile, under %APPDATA%\HelpDesk Academy. Installing a newer version, or uninstalling, keeps it.
